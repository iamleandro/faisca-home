Signal Lock — state colours

HUE IS THE
SECOND CHANNEL

Every state carries a marker shape and a luminance step as well as a colour, so the tuner still reads with deuteranopia, with protanopia, and on a phone in daylight.

IDLE
--game-idle  #6E7A70  · 4.17:1
No signal within range. Thin 2px needle, no glow, no ring. The band ticks stay at --color-line.
NEAR
--game-near  #DA8D23  · 6.96:1
Needle is inside the catch radius. Lock window draws as a wide outline; needle thickens to 3px and warms.
LOCKING
--game-locking  #FBB924  · 10.7:1
Holding. The window contracts over ~1.2 s and the lock meter under the band fills left-to-right. Window edges pulse at 2 Hz.
LOCKED
--game-locked  #6BE04D  · 11.0:1
Lead logged. Window snaps shut and fills, spark burst fires from the needle head, dispatch line prints below the stage.
LOST
--game-lost  #C4402A  · 3.66:1
Needle slipped out before the hold completed. Window and needle both break into dashes — the dash pattern is what reads, not the red.
PHANTOM
--game-phantom  #6B655C  · 3.24:1
Decoy. Hollow dashed diamond, never a filled lozenge, and it jitters ±2px at 8 Hz. Locking one costs 3 s off the clock.
Where colour alone is not enough

Three pairs in this ramp sit close in luminance. Colour cannot carry them, so each is separated by form. This is stated rather than hidden because the coding agent has to implement the form, not just the token.

locking / locked
1.03
sequential, never co-present · outline → filled + burst
near / phantom
2.15
co-present · filled lozenge vs dashed hollow diamond
locked / lost
3.01
solid stroke vs 7-7 dash · clears 3:1 on its own

The obvious green/red pair measured 1.58:1 against each other — indistinguishable without colour vision. Locked moved up to #6BE04D and lost moved down to #C4402A, which puts 3.01:1 between them while both still clear 3:1 on the stage black.
