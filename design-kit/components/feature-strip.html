One round is shorter than the queue for a coffee. Beat your own number.

The 1 px separators are the grid container's background showing through a gap: 1px — cells carry no borders of their own, so neighbours never stack two hairlines into a 2 px line. At 820 px three columns would leave ~180 px of content each, which is too narrow for the condensed display face, so tablets get 2-up and the third cell wraps. There is no hover state: these are not links, and a hover affordance on a non-interactive block is a lie.
