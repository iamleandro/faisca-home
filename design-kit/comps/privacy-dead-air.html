Dead Air: Ashmere

Privacy Policy

Dead Air: Ashmere (“Dead Air” or the “game”) is operated by Faisca Games Inc. (“Faisca Games,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), a Canadian corporation based in Ontario.

Effective date: [Publication date] · Last updated: [Publication date]

This Privacy Policy explains what information Dead Air processes, why it is processed, when it is shared, how long it is retained, and the choices and rights available to you. It applies only to the Dead Air: Ashmere application, not the general Faisca Games website or any other game, product, or service.

1. Summary

Dead Air is a narrative investigation game with an in-game credit system and optional purchases processed through Apple’s App Store.

We do not:

  • sell or rent personal information;
  • show third-party advertising;
  • use third-party advertising SDKs;
  • share information with data brokers;
  • track you across apps or websites owned by other companies for advertising; or
  • receive your payment-card or bank-account details.

We use services provided by Google Firebase and Google Cloud to operate accounts, synchronize game entitlements and credits, protect the service, understand game performance when you consent to Analytics, and diagnose crashes.

If you request a refund from Apple, you may separately choose whether Dead Air can send limited credit-consumption information to Apple to assist with Apple’s refund decision. This sharing is optional.

2. Information we process

2.1 Game accounts and authentication

Dead Air creates an anonymous Firebase account when the game starts. This account uses a randomly generated Firebase user identifier. We use it to associate your account with game credits, purchases, unlocked content, consent settings, and related server-side records.

The game may also create a randomly generated app-account token that allows an App Store purchase to be associated with the correct Dead Air account.

If you link your account using Sign in with Apple, Firebase stores an association between your Dead Air account and your Apple sign-in identifier.

Dead Air does not request your name or email address from Apple as part of Sign in with Apple.

Authentication tokens and authorization credentials may be transmitted to Apple and Firebase to complete authentication or account revocation. Dead Air does not intentionally retain raw Apple identity tokens or Apple authorization codes in its game database.

2.2 Game and economy information

We process information needed to operate the game and its credit system. This may include:

  • your current credit balance;
  • credit grants and spending;
  • credit-refill timing;
  • unlocked cases or content;
  • purchase entitlements;
  • product identifiers;
  • limited gameplay and progression events;
  • internal case, scene, transcript, evidence, dialogue, or content identifiers;
  • categorized outcomes and errors; and
  • information needed to reconcile credits following a refund.

We do not use your game activity or story choices to create an advertising profile.

Some save information, preferences, language selections, and game state may be stored locally on your device. Information that remains exclusively on your device is not collected by us.

Removing the app normally removes information stored in the app’s local container, subject to Apple’s operating-system behavior and any device backups.

2.3 Purchases and refunds

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment information such as card or bank-account details. Faisca Games does not receive those details.

To verify purchases, grant credits, prevent duplicate grants, and process refunds, we may process:

  • Apple transaction identifiers;
  • product identifiers;
  • purchase and refund dates;
  • the number of credits purchased, granted, spent, or refunded;
  • StoreKit environment information, such as Sandbox or Production;
  • an app-account token used to associate a purchase with your game account;
  • purchase-validation status;
  • notification and refund status; and
  • limited audit records needed to prevent transaction replay and maintain the integrity of the credit system.

Raw signed StoreKit transaction data may be processed during validation but is not intentionally retained in the Dead Air game database.

2.4 Optional information shared with Apple for refund decisions

If Apple notifies Dead Air of a refund request, the game may ask for your permission to send limited consumption information to Apple.

If you consent, Dead Air may send Apple:

  • the relevant Apple transaction identifier;
  • confirmation that you consented to the sharing;
  • an approximate percentage of the purchased credits that were consumed;
  • a delivery-status value; and
  • whether sample content was provided.

Dead Air does not include your Firebase user identifier, email address, Apple app-account token, raw signed transaction, or detailed gameplay history in this report.

Apple—not Faisca Games—makes the refund decision. Apple may consider the consumption information together with other information available to Apple.

You can enable or disable “Share Credit Usage with Apple” in the game’s Settings. Disabling it prevents future consumption reports from being sent. It cannot recall information that has already been delivered to Apple.

If you do not consent, no consumption report is sent to Apple for that refund request. This consent is separate from consent for Firebase Analytics.

2.5 Analytics information

Dead Air uses Firebase Analytics only if you allow Analytics collection.

Analytics is off unless you turn it on. You can enable or disable it at any time using the Share anonymous usage data switch in Settings → Privacy. Turning it off stops collection and clears the analytics identifier held on your device.

When enabled, Analytics may process:

  • app version and build;
  • operating-system information;
  • device model or category;
  • a Firebase installation or app-instance identifier;
  • an Apple Identifier for Vendor, where made available to the Analytics SDK;
  • internal screen, case, content, or product identifiers;
  • generalized credit-balance ranges;
  • feature interactions and completion events;
  • timing and performance measurements;
  • categorized failure reasons;
  • network information, such as an IP address; and
  • an approximate region inferred by the service from network information.

We do not intentionally include names, email addresses, support messages, story dialogue, raw Apple transaction identifiers, or raw signed purchase data in Analytics events.

Dead Air does not use Analytics for personalized advertising or cross-company tracking. Advertising-personalization signals are disabled. The game does not intentionally include an IDFA-capable Analytics configuration.

Turning Analytics off stops future Analytics collection from the app. Information already collected remains subject to the retention and deletion processes described in this policy.

2.6 Crash and diagnostic information

We use Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose crashes and technical failures.

Crash and diagnostic reports may include:

  • stack traces and technical error details;
  • app version and build;
  • operating-system version;
  • device model;
  • a Firebase installation identifier;
  • the screen, case, action, or internal content identifier associated with a failure;
  • limited application state needed to understand a failure; and
  • recent Analytics events used as diagnostic breadcrumbs, if Analytics was enabled when those events occurred.

We do not intentionally attach your name, email address, support-message text, raw Apple transaction identifier, or raw signed purchase data to a crash report.

Crashlytics is used for the reliability and security of the game rather than advertising.

2.7 App and device integrity

We use Firebase App Check and Apple device-attestation services to help distinguish legitimate copies of the game from automated, modified, or abusive clients.

These services may process:

  • Firebase installation identifiers;
  • app-attestation tokens;
  • device and application integrity signals;
  • network information; and
  • related technical metadata.

These signals are used to protect accounts, purchases, credits, and backend services. They are not used for advertising.

2.8 Support information

If you select “Report a Problem,” Dead Air prepares a support message in your device’s email application. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send the message.

The prepared message may include:

  • information that you enter;
  • app version and build;
  • operating-system version;
  • device model;
  • selected language;
  • the current screen or case;
  • internal content identifiers; and
  • limited displayed or localized content needed to explain the problem.

Your email address and message are processed by your chosen email provider and received by Faisca Games only if you send the message.

If an email application is unavailable and you choose to copy the report, the report is placed on your device’s clipboard. Other applications or services with access to the clipboard may be able to read it, subject to your device settings and operating-system controls.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • create and authenticate game accounts;
  • associate Sign in with Apple with a game account;
  • synchronize credits, unlocks, and entitlements;
  • verify purchases;
  • grant purchased credits;
  • apply and reconcile refunds;
  • prevent duplicate or fraudulent transaction use;
  • operate and secure backend services;
  • protect the credit system from abuse;
  • save or restore eligible game progress and unlocks;
  • measure reliability and feature usage when Analytics consent is enabled;
  • diagnose crashes and technical problems;
  • respond to support and privacy requests;
  • process account-deletion requests;
  • maintain limited transaction-integrity records; and
  • send consumption information to Apple when you have separately consented.

Depending on applicable law and the activity involved, we process information to provide the game and purchased services, based on your consent, to protect the security and integrity of the service, or to comply with applicable legal requirements.

Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw that consent through the controls described in this policy. Withdrawal does not affect processing that occurred before consent was withdrawn.

4. When information is shared

We share information only as needed to operate and protect Dead Air.

Google Firebase and Google Cloud

We use services that may include Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Firebase App Check, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Cloud Logging, and related Google Cloud infrastructure.

Google processes information according to applicable service terms and Faisca Games’ configuration and instructions. More information is available in Google’s Firebase Privacy and Security documentation.

Apple

Apple may process information in connection with Sign in with Apple, App Store purchases, StoreKit transaction validation, App Store Server Notifications, refunds, optional consumption reports, device attestation, and App Store operation.

Information Apple receives is also subject to Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Email and support providers

If you voluntarily send a support or privacy request, the message is processed by your selected email provider and the communication services used by Faisca Games.

Legal disclosures

We may disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, a binding legal requirement, or valid legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of players, Faisca Games, or others.

We do not sell or rent personal information, share it with data brokers, or share it with advertising networks.

5. Retention

Active account and economy records

Player, account, balance, entitlement, and active economy records are retained while the account exists. Account-associated records are removed through the account-deletion process, subject to the limited records described below.

Purchase lots and economy ledger

Purchase lots and economy-ledger records are retained while needed to operate the account, verify balances, process spending, and reconcile refunds.

Account-associated purchase lots and ledger records are deleted when the account is deleted, except for limited audit and replay-prevention records.

Compact purchase and refund audit records

A compact purchase or refund audit record may be retained for up to 24 months after the later applicable deletion or refund event.

These compact records are designed not to contain a Firebase user identifier, an Apple app-account token, a raw Apple transaction identifier, a raw signed transaction, or detailed gameplay history.

Transaction replay-prevention fingerprints

A keyed, non-reversible transaction fingerprint may be retained while the purchase service remains operational. This prevents the same Apple transaction from being claimed more than once.

If the purchase service is permanently discontinued, these fingerprints are targeted for removal within 24 months.

Consent records

The current refund-report consent setting is retained while the account exists.

Account-associated consent records are deleted when the account is deleted.

Consumption reports sent to Apple

Terminal consumption reports successfully sent to Apple may be retained for up to 24 months.

Consumption reports not sent to Apple

Terminal consumption reports that were not sent to Apple are retained for no longer than 90 days. Terminal unsent records are designed not to retain the player’s Firebase user identifier or raw transaction identifier.

Apple notification deduplication records

Terminal records used to ensure that an Apple server notification is not processed more than once may be retained for up to 180 days.

Deletion tombstones

A keyed, non-reversible deletion fingerprint may be retained for up to seven days after authentication deletion. This allows account cleanup to resume safely if part of the deletion process is temporarily interrupted.

Firebase Analytics

User-level and event-level Firebase Analytics data is configured for a two-month retention period.

Google Analytics retention controls may not affect previously generated aggregated reports. Turning Analytics off stops future collection but does not immediately remove previously generated aggregate statistics.

Firebase Crashlytics

Google states that Crashlytics stack traces, minidumps, and associated identifiers are retained for 90 days before the removal process begins.

Application and function logs

Ordinary application and function logs are targeted for retention for no longer than 30 days.

Provider-required audit logs may be retained by Google Cloud for up to 400 days where that retention period is controlled by the service and cannot be shortened by Faisca Games.

We avoid intentionally writing raw Apple transaction identifiers, authentication credentials, support-message content, or other unnecessary personal information into application logs.

Backups

Backups containing Dead Air data are retained for no longer than seven days.

Deleted information may remain in a protected backup until that backup expires. Backups are not used to restore individual deleted accounts. If a backup is restored for disaster recovery, deletion records must be reapplied before the restored system is returned to normal service.

Support and privacy emails

Support and privacy-request emails are retained for no longer than 90 days and are deleted manually.

A message may be deleted sooner following a verified deletion request, unless it is still reasonably needed to complete the request, resolve an active support matter, protect the service, or comply with an applicable legal requirement.

Information held by other providers

Information processed independently by Apple, Google, an email provider, or another service may be retained according to that provider’s policies and legal obligations.

6. Your choices

Analytics

Analytics is off unless you turn it on. You can enable or disable it at any time using the Share anonymous usage data switch in Settings → Privacy. Turning it off stops collection and clears the analytics identifier held on your device.

Leaving Analytics off does not prevent you from playing the game or using paid functionality.

Sharing credit usage with Apple

You can enable or disable “Share Credit Usage with Apple” in Settings.

Disabling this option prevents future consumption reports from being sent to Apple. It does not recall information previously delivered to Apple.

Declining this sharing does not prevent you from requesting a refund from Apple. Apple independently determines whether a refund is approved.

Account deletion

You can request account deletion from Dead Air’s Settings.

Account deletion removes the primary Firebase account and account-associated player, economy, purchase-lot, ledger, and consent records. Pending consumption-report records are de-linked from the account and expire automatically under the retention periods described in this policy. Limited pseudonymous audit, security, logging, backup, or replay-prevention records may remain for the periods described in this policy.

If the account is linked with Apple, Dead Air may require you to reauthenticate before deletion and will attempt to revoke the Apple authorization associated with the account.

Account deletion does not delete purchase or refund information maintained independently by Apple.

Account deletion is permanent. Remaining credits, account-specific entitlements, and server-side progress associated with the deleted account may be lost.

7. Privacy requests and account verification

You may contact Faisca Games to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with your Dead Air account.

To protect account information, we must verify that you control the relevant account.

When available, you can generate a one-time Privacy Request Code from Dead Air’s Settings while authenticated. The code:

  • is random and single-use;
  • is valid only for a privacy request;
  • expires after seven days; and
  • is stored by Faisca Games only in a protected, hashed form.

Send that code with your request to support@faisca.gg.

Do not send us an Apple receipt, a raw Apple transaction identifier, an identity token, an authentication credential, payment-card information, or government identification unless Faisca Games specifically determines that it is legally necessary and provides secure instructions.

If you no longer have access to a completely anonymous account and cannot generate a valid Privacy Request Code, Faisca Games may be unable to locate or verify the account safely. This prevents another person from accessing or deleting an account they do not control.

We will respond to verified requests as required by applicable law.

  • confirm whether we process your personal information;
  • request access to personal information;
  • request correction of incomplete or inaccurate information;
  • request deletion of eligible information;
  • withdraw consent;

  • receive information about service providers or other recipients;

These rights may be subject to legal exceptions. We may retain limited information where necessary to protect the service, prevent transaction replay, complete a request, or meet an applicable legal requirement.

To exercise a privacy right, contact support@faisca.gg.

United States

Canada

Public release in Canada is subject to Faisca Games completing its regional legal and language review.

A Brazilian Portuguese version of this policy and the relevant in-app notices will be made available before Dead Air is publicly released in Brazil.

9. Children’s privacy

Dead Air is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13 and will not be offered in Apple’s Kids Category.

If you are under the age of majority where you live, you should use the game only with the involvement of a parent or guardian.

We do not knowingly solicit a child’s name, email address, physical address, photograph, precise location, or other directly identifying information through the game.

If we learn that personal information from a child under 13 has been processed contrary to applicable requirements, a parent or guardian may contact support@faisca.gg so that we can investigate and delete eligible information.

When processing information relating to a minor, we will consider the minor’s best interests as required by applicable law.

  • database access controls;
  • server-side transaction verification;
  • device and application attestation;
  • keyed, non-reversible identifiers;
  • limited retention periods;
  • deletion workflows;

No storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. International processing

Faisca Games is located in Canada. Google, Apple, and other service providers may process information in Canada, the United States, or other countries where they or their service providers operate.

The privacy and data-protection laws in those countries may differ from those where you live. Where required, Faisca Games relies on applicable contracts, safeguards, and legal mechanisms for international processing.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game, its service providers, its data practices, or applicable requirements change.

We will update the “Last updated” date when changes are published. We will provide additional notice when required by applicable law.

If a material change affects processing based on your consent, we will request any new consent required before applying the change to that processing.
