control states — default · held · disabled · focus
LOG is 64 px tall and takes the full width between the arrows because it is held, not tapped — a held control needs somewhere forgiving to rest a thumb. No fake status bar and no fake keyboard: the real ones render on top, and the layout reserves env(safe-area-inset-bottom) instead. The dial drag surface is the whole upper stage, not a thin strip, so a thumb anywhere above the meter tunes. Set touch-action: none on both or the browser will steal the drag for scrolling.
