44 × 44 · aria-pressed carries the state
Everything here is DOM over the canvas, never text drawn into the bitmap — it stays selectable, translatable, zoomable and readable to a screen reader. Score and timer live in an aria-live="polite" region that announces only on a lock and at 10 seconds, not every frame. The timer turns --game-lost under 10 s, which is a colour change on top of a number that was already there, not colour as the only signal. Reduce effects removes the scanlines, the needle glow and the spark burst; it does not change any gameplay timing.
