DEAD AIR.

Sixty seconds, nothing logged. The band was busy — you were not.
Hold LOG. The window will not close on its own.
Veil opacity is the hierarchy: 0.80 pause (you are checking the clock, the HUD must stay readable), 0.87 start and round end, 0.93 fail (nothing behind it is worth reading). The fail screen shows no score row at all — a row of zeroes is a scolding. Each screen sets role="dialog" aria-modal="true", moves focus to its primary button, and traps Tab; the game loop pauses while any of them is open.
